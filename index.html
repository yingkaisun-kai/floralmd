FloralMD · iOS & macOS

Two platforms, each with a clear focus.

FloralMD offers separate native Markdown products for iOS and macOS. Each edition is distributed independently and has its own features and data practices.

FloralMD for iOS

Local, offline reading

A read-only Markdown reader offered through the Apple App Store. Import a document from Files or another app and keep an offline copy on your device without modifying the source file.

Support Privacy Policy

FloralMD for macOS

Independently distributed and open source

A native editor for local Markdown files with inline live preview. The macOS edition is not covered by this website’s iOS Privacy Policy.
